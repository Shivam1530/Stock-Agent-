# Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE): research brief
*As of 23 Sep 2026. Built from the scraped pack only; not investment advice.*

## 1. Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables makes power cables, wires and conductors [S4]. In Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026) revenue was ₹1,248 crore (+18% YoY), but EBITDA (operating profit) grew only 2.6% to ₹140 crore, so the margin fell from 12.9% to 11.2%. Profit after tax was ₹82 crore (+5.9%) [S4]. Order book is ₹3,900 crore vs ₹3,150 crore a year ago [S4]. Management kept FY27 revenue growth guidance at 15–17% [S3].

## 2. Bull case
- **Visible demand:** order book up ~24% YoY (derived: 3,900/3,150) and about 3x one quarter's revenue (derived: 3,900/1,248) [S4].
- **New growth engines:** data-centre cables are ~9% of domestic revenue; management thinks that can double in two years (its view) [S3]. Exports rose to 21% of revenue from 16% [S4].
- **Capacity coming:** the Bharuch plant adds ~30% cable capacity from Q3 FY27 [S4]. ~70% of its ₹620 crore capex is spent; net debt/equity is a modest 0.35x [S3].
- **Margin dip may be temporary (management's claim):** "mostly copper"; ~two-thirds of contracts pass on copper price changes, with a 1–2 quarter lag [S3, S4].
- **Promoter pledge falling:** pledged shares dropped from 11.5% (Jun 2025) to 6.0% (Mar 2026) to 4.1% of promoter holding. FII (foreign funds) rose 9.2% → 11.8% and DII (Indian funds) 12.7% → 14.6% [S2].

## 3. Bear case
- **Profits lag sales:** revenue +18% but EBITDA +2.6% and PAT +5.9% [S4]. About one-third of contracts have no copper price-adjustment clause (derived from "two-thirds do") [S3], so recovery is not assured.
- **Cash getting stuck:** receivable days (time to collect payment) rose from 78 to 96 in a year, mainly two large state utilities paying slowly. No defaults so far [S3].
- **Tax dispute:** a ₹46.3 crore GST demand (FY21–FY22 input tax credit, incl. interest and penalty) was received on 2 Sep 2026. The company plans to appeal and expects no material impact (its own view) [S1]. For scale, it is ~56% of one quarter's profit (derived: 46.3/82) [S4].
- **Execution risk:** guidance leans on Bharuch contributing from H2 [S3]; a delay would hit growth.

## 4. Open questions
1. **Revenue conflict:** the company says ₹1,248 crore (+18%) [S4]; Business Daily says ₹1,428 crore (+35%) [S6], and a tips blog repeats +35% [S8]. I used S4: stated EBITDA ₹140 crore at an 11.2% margin implies ~₹1,250 crore revenue (derived: 140/0.112), while ₹1,428 crore would give 9.8% (derived: 140/1,428). S6 looks like a digit transposition. Check the NSE filing.
2. **GST:** appeal timeline, provisioning and exposure for other years are not in the sources [S1].
3. **Receivables:** the two utilities' share of sales and the age of dues are not disclosed [S3].
4. **Copper pass-through:** no Q2 FY27 data yet to show margin recovery. The "nearly 14%" copper rise comes only from S6, which has a revenue error [S6].
5. **Valuation:** no current price or P/E; the only price is ₹1,012 on 7 Aug, six weeks old [S6]. Cheap or expensive cannot be judged.
6. **Pledge:** what drove the fall? Promoter holding is flat at ~58% [S2], so it is unclear how loans were repaid.
7. **Excluded:** S5 is a different firm (Sarvottam Cable Network, a Nagpur cable-TV operator). S7 (Mar 2024, 35% pledge) is superseded by S2.
