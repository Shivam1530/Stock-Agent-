# Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE): research brief
*As of 2026-09-23. Sources scraped by pipeline; not investment advice.*

## 1. Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd is a manufacturer of power cables, wires, and conductors based in India. In Q1 FY27, the company reported a 18% increase in revenue to ₹1,248 crore, driven by strong demand from power transmission and data-centre projects. EBITDA was up 2.6% at ₹140 crore, but margins contracted by 170 bps due to rising copper prices. The company’s order book stands at ₹3,900 crore.

## 2. Bull case
- **Robust Revenue Growth:** The company’s revenue increased by 18% year-on-year to ₹1,248 crore (S4), fueled by sustained demand in key sectors like power transmission and data centres. This indicates a healthy market position and growth trajectory.
- **Capacity Expansion Catalyst:** The upcoming commercial operations of the new Bharuch (Gujarat) plant, slated to begin in Q3 FY27, will add approximately 30% to cable capacity (S4), providing a significant growth catalyst for future revenue.
- **Export Market Diversification:**  Exports now contribute 21% to revenue (S4), demonstrating a successful diversification strategy and potential for further expansion beyond the domestic market.

## 3. Bear case
- **Margin Pressure from Commodity Prices:** EBITDA margins contracted by 170 bps to 11.2% (S3, S4) due to rising copper prices, a significant raw material cost, posing a challenge to profitability.
- **Elevated Promoter Pledge:** The promoter pledge has increased to 35% (S7), raising concerns about potential liquidity pressures if the stock price declines and highlighting a potential governance risk.
- **Increased Receivable Days:**  A rise in receivable days to 96 days (S3) due to delayed payments from large utility customers indicates potential operational challenges and liquidity risks requiring closer monitoring.

## 4. Open questions
1. **Revenue Reconciliation:** There is a significant discrepancy between the reported revenue of ₹1,248 crore (S4) and the reported revenue of ₹1,428 crore (S6). The company’s EBITDA/revenue calculation (9.8% vs. 11.2%) requires further investigation into the underlying drivers of revenue and margin fluctuations. [S3, S6]
2. **Valuation Uncertainty:** The current market price of SRVCABLE is not available in the provided sources. A full-year earnings forecast and valuation analysis are needed to determine a fair value.
3. **Margin Recovery Timeline:** The company’s expectation to pass on higher copper costs over the next two quarters is uncertain. The timing and magnitude of margin recovery remain unclear and require closer monitoring. [S3]
4. **Receivable Days Root Cause:** The increase in receivable days (96 days) warrants further investigation to understand the specific reasons behind the delays, particularly concerning the delayed payments from large utility customers. [S3]
