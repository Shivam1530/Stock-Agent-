# Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE): research brief
*As of 2026-09-23. Sources scraped by pipeline; not investment advice.*

## 1. Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd is a manufacturer of power cables, wires, and conductors [S4]. The company reported Q1 FY27 results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, showing revenue from operations of ₹1,248 crore, marking an 18.0% year-over-year (YoY) increase [S4]. While the order book grew significantly to ₹3,900 crore [S4], the EBITDA margin contracted by 170 basis points (bps) to 11.2% due to rising copper prices [S4].

## 2. Bull case
The strongest arguments for the company center on sustained demand and capacity expansion:

1. **Strong Order Book Growth:** The order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of June 30, 2026, representing a substantial increase from ₹3,150 crore the previous year [S4].
2. **Benefiting from Infrastructure Trends:** Demand remains strong from power transmission and data-centre projects [S4]. The company notes that data-centre cables now account for about 9% of its domestic revenue, with management expecting this segment to double in two years [S3].
3. **Capacity Expansion and Exports:** The new Bharuch plant is expected to add about 30% to the company's cable capacity [S4]. Furthermore, exports contributed 21% of the Q1 revenue, up from 16% in Q1 FY26 [S4].

## 3. Bear case
Potential risks relate to commodity pricing, working capital, and regulatory exposure:

1. **Margin Pressure from Commodities:** The EBITDA margin contracted by 170 bps to 11.2% [S4]. Management attributes this to a sharp rise in copper prices, which they expect to pass on to customers over the next two quarters [S4].
2. **Increased Receivables:** The company’s receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year [S3]. This increase is attributed to two large state utility customers where payment cycles are slower [S3].
3. **Regulatory and Legal Exposure:** The company received a GST demand order for ₹46.3 crore relating to input tax credit claimed for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 [S1].

## 4. Open questions
1. **Revenue Conflict [S4][S6]:** There is a conflict regarding the reported Q1 FY27 revenue. The highest-tier source (T2, S4) reports revenue of ₹1,248 crore (18.0% YoY growth), while a news source (T3, S6) reports ₹1,428 crore (35% growth). The T2 source is preferred, but the discrepancy must be noted.
2. **Valuation and Full-Year Guidance:** The sources do not provide a current valuation metric (e.g., P/E ratio) or a definitive full-year financial guidance for FY27, making it difficult to assess if the current price reflects future growth [S3][S4].
3. **Impact of GST Demand:** While the company states it does not expect a material impact from the ₹46.3 crore GST demand [S1], the potential financial and legal costs associated with the appeal process are not quantified.
4. **Working Capital Cycle:** The sources do not provide a clear plan or timeline for reducing the increased receivable days (96 days) [S3].
