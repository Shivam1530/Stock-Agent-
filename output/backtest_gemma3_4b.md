# Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE): research brief
*As of 2026-09-23. Sources scraped by pipeline; not investment advice.*

## 1. Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd is a manufacturer of power cables, wires, and conductors based in India. The company announced Q1 FY27 results with a 18% revenue increase to ₹1,248 crore, driven by strong demand in power transmission and data-centre projects. However, EBITDA margins contracted by 170 bps due to rising copper prices. The company holds a significant promoter stake (58.2%) and has a robust order book of ₹3,900 crore.

## 2. Bull case
- **Strong Demand Drivers:** The company is benefiting from significant demand in the power transmission and data-centre sectors, evidenced by a strong order book of ₹3,900 crore [S4]. This suggests continued growth potential.
- **Capacity Expansion:** The new Bharuch (Gujarat) plant, adding about 30% to cable capacity, is slated to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27 [S4], further supporting revenue growth.
- **Export Growth:** Exports contributed 21% of revenue, up from 16% the previous year [S4], demonstrating the company’s ability to tap into international markets.

## 3. Bear case
- **Margin Pressure:** Rising copper prices (approximately 14% during Q1 FY27) significantly impacted EBITDA margins, contracting by 170 bps [S6]. The MD acknowledged this, but the impact is likely to persist in the short term.
- **Promoter Pledge:** The promoter pledge has increased to 35% [S7], raising governance concerns and potentially limiting the company’s ability to raise capital.
- **Receivable Days Increase:** Increased receivable days (from 78 to 96) due to large utility customer payments highlight potential liquidity risks [S3].

## 4. Open questions
1.  **Valuation:** A full-year revenue forecast and detailed valuation metrics are missing, making it difficult to assess the company’s fair value. The implied revenue from S4’s EBITDA and revenue figures is ₹1,250 crore, but this is only for Q1 FY27.
2.  **Margin Recovery:** The timeline for margin recovery is unclear. The MD expects margins to improve over two quarters, but this is not guaranteed. The source states the MD expects margins to improve over two quarters, but does not quantify this.
3.  **GST Demand:** The ₹46.3 crore GST demand remains unresolved [S1]. The company believes it has a strong case on merits, based on legal advice, and intends to file an appeal before the appropriate authority, but the outcome is uncertain.
4.  **Shareholding Pattern:** The 4.1% promoter holding pledged to lenders [S2] raises concerns about potential liquidity issues.
5. **Nagpur Penalty:** The ₹8 lakh penalty for illegal overhead wiring [S5] is a minor issue, but highlights potential regulatory risks.
6. **Revenue Reconciliation:** There is a conflict between the reported revenue of ₹1,428 crore [S6] and the company’s stated revenue of ₹1,248 crore [S4]. The discrepancy needs clarification.
