# Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE): research brief
*As of 2026-09-23. Sources scraped by pipeline; not investment advice.*

## 1. Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd is a manufacturer of power cables, wires, and conductors [S4]. The company reported Q1 FY27 results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, showing revenue from operations of ₹1,248 crore, representing an 18.0% increase year-over-year (YoY) [S4]. While demand remains strong, the company faced margin pressure due to a sharp rise in copper costs, causing the EBITDA margin to contract by 170 basis points (bps) to 11.2% [S4].

## 2. Bull case
- **Strong Demand Drivers:** Demand remains robust, particularly from power transmission and data-centre projects [S4]. The company’s order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of June 30, 2026, indicating strong future visibility [S4].
- **Capacity Expansion:** The new Bharuch plant in Gujarat is adding approximately 30% to the company's cable capacity [S4]. This expansion is expected to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27 [S4].
- **International Growth:** Exports contributed 21% of the Q1 FY27 revenue, showing increasing reliance on international markets [S4].

## 3. Bear case
- **Commodity Price Headwinds:** The EBITDA margin contracted by 170 bps to 11.2% [S4]. Management attributed this contraction to a sharp rise in copper prices during the quarter, which they expect to pass on to customers over the next two quarters [S4].
- **Working Capital Strain:** The company’s receivable days increased from 78 to 96 days over the last year [S3]. This increase is mainly attributed to slower payments from two large state utility customers [S3].
- **Legal and Governance Risks:** The company received a GST demand order of ₹46.3 crore (including interest and penalty) related to input tax credit claimed for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 [S1]. Furthermore, while the current promoter pledged shareholding is 4.1% of the promoter holding [S2], historical data shows this has been a point of concern [S7].

## 4. Open questions
- **1. Revenue Conflict:** There is a conflict regarding the Q1 FY27 revenue figure. The highest-tier source (T2, S4) reports ₹1,248 crore (+18.0% YoY), while a news source (T3, S6) reports ₹1,428 crore (+35%) [S6]. The reason for this discrepancy and which figure is accurate is unclear.
- **2. Valuation and Full-Year Guidance:** The sources provide Q1 results and a full-year revenue guidance range (15-17% growth for FY27) [S3], but they do not provide a current valuation multiple (e.g., P/E ratio) or a definitive full-year profit guidance for 2027.
- **3. Materiality of GST Demand:** While the company states it does not expect a material impact from the ₹46.3 crore GST demand [S1], the potential impact of this legal challenge on cash flow remains unquantified.
